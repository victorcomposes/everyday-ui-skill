What counts as a monitor?

One URL, port or heartbeat that Relay checks. Multi-step API checks count as one monitor.

Can I change plan later?

Yes. Upgrades apply at once and downgrades apply at the end of the billing period.

What happens if I go over my limit?

Existing monitors keep running. You cannot add more until you upgrade or remove some.

Do you offer refunds?

Annual plans have a 30-day refund window. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time.
