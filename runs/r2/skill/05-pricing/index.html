Questions about billing

Something not covered here? Email billing@relay-monitoring.example and a person replies within one business day.

How does Relay count monitors?

One monitor is one URL, host or port that Relay checks on a schedule. A monitor checked from five regions is still one monitor.

What happens if I reach my monitor limit?

Existing monitors keep running. Relay blocks new monitors until you pause one or move to the next plan, and it emails the account owner when you reach 90% of the limit.

Can I change plan or cancel at any time?

Yes. Upgrades apply at once and you pay the difference for the rest of the period. Downgrades and cancellations apply at the end of the period you have paid for.

Do prices include VAT?

No. Prices are in pounds sterling and exclude VAT. Relay adds VAT at your local rate at checkout and shows it on every invoice.

Is there a trial for paid plans?

Team and Business start with a 14-day trial. You do not need a card to start, and Relay does not charge you until you choose to continue.

Know before your customers do

Every plan runs the same checks on the same network. Higher plans check more often and from more regions, so an outage shows up in seconds.
