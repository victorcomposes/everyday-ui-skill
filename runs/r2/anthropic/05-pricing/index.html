Questions we hear before people sign up

What counts as a monitor?

One monitor is one endpoint check, such as a GET on /health. A multi-step check that calls three endpoints in order still counts as one monitor.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades apply at once and we credit the unused part of your current period. Downgrades start at your next renewal.

What happens if I go over my monitor limit?

Existing monitors keep running. You pay $0.40 for each extra monitor on Team and Scale, or you can pause the ones you do not need. Hobby stops you at 5.

Do you offer a refund?

Yearly plans have a 30-day refund window. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and are not charged again.
