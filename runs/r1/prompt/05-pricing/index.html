Questions, answered

What counts as a monitor?

One monitor is one URL or API test. A multi-step test that calls five endpoints in order is still one monitor.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades apply at once and we prorate the difference. Downgrades take effect at the end of your billing period.

Do you charge per seat?

No. Invite your whole team on every plan. Pricing depends only on monitors, interval and features.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your monitors pause and your data stays for 30 days. Pick a plan to resume, or export everything as CSV.
