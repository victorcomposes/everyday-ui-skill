Winner: skill.

  • opus2 desktop-fold.png a red-outlined Remove button on every row, the owner included, makes the destructive action the loudest thing on the page
  • sonnet1 dark.png identical to the light page, so there is no dark mode at all; the mobile cards are also an unstyled stack of plain 'Active' text and default selects
  • sonnet2 mobile-fold.png the Team nav tab is clipped to Tea beside the avatar

  • opus2 mobile-fold.png the active nav tab is clipped to Tea by the avatar, and Last active disappears from the member cards
  • sonnet1 mobile-fold.png the Team nav tab is clipped to 'Tea' at the right edge, and each member card stacks a full-width role select, trash icon and status badge into a tall, loose block
  • sonnet2 mobile-fold.png each person stacks name, email, select, status and a red Remove button, about 370px per member; dark.png has no dark mode

anthropic

  • opus1 desktop.png the white sidebar background stops at 900px and leaves the rest of the left column bare grey
  • opus2 desktop.png the white sidebar background ends at 900 px, leaving a grey block below it
  • sonnet1 dark.png identical to the light page, so no dark mode; the saturated blue invite banner also outweighs the member list it sits above, and on mobile its stacked 'Invite / as' labels fill the whole first screen
  • sonnet2 desktop-fold.png the oversized Invite ... as ... Send invite banner dominates the page and pushes the member list down; dark.png is identical to light

  • opus2 desktop-fold.png rows are tight and every avatar has the same blue tint, so 25 rows read as an undifferentiated wall
  • sonnet1 mobile-fold.png the role, status and last-active row is loosely aligned, with the timestamp floating at an arbitrary offset and the three items not on a shared grid

Method in short

  • Arms: baseline (no instructions), prompt (a strong designer instruction), anthropic (Anthropic's frontend-design skill) and skill (everyday-ui).
  • Every page is one index.html. It follows the contract in evals/RULES.md.
  • Labels are shuffled per task by seed. Judges saw five screenshots per page: desktop fold, desktop full, phone fold, phone full and dark.
  • Judges score nine criteria from 1 to 10 and name the worst defect. From round r2 on, overall judges light mode only.
  • A separate lint measures axe, overflow at 390 px, tap targets under 44 px and more. It does not use the judges.
  • Arm names on this page are unblinded.

Caveats: one sample per arm per task. All judges are Claude models. The anthropic arm's agent also screenshotted its own pages. No human has judged these pages. Absolute scores do not carry across rounds. Read docs/METHOD.md for the full list and docs/LESSONS.md for what moved scores.
